15 Years of Freshness, Quality & Trust

For the past 15 years, Shri Saibaba Associates has been proudly serving the fresh produce industry, building lasting relationships through quality, reliability and dedicated service.

We specialize in supplying a wide range of fresh and exotic vegetables to hotels, restaurants, cafés, caterers, supermarkets, retailers and other businesses across Hyderabad and Telangana.

Our philosophy is simple: fresh produce, dependable service and relationships built to last. We understand that professional kitchens depend on consistency, which is why we focus on carefully sourced produce, freshness, hygienic handling and reliable supply.

“More than a supplier — a trusted fresh-produce partner for every kitchen we serve.”
